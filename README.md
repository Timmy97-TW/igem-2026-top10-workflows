# iGEM 2026 Top 10 Workflow Atlas / iGEM 2026 前十名工作流程圖譜

**[→ Read the site](https://timmy97-tw.github.io/igem-2026-top10-workflows/)**

How the thirty teams named to the **Top 10 High School**, **Top 10 Undergraduate** and **Top 10
Overgraduate** lists at the iGEM 2026 Grand Jamboree actually ran their wet lab and their dry lab.
Three sections, one overview and one comparison page each, one page per team, read from each team's
own public 2026 wiki — plus an **Across tiers** section that reads all thirty against each other and
turns them into a project-selection guide for 2027. Bilingual (English / 繁體中文), figure-led, no
build step, no dependencies.

Compiled by **GEMS Research (Taiwan)**.

一份關於 iGEM 2026 Grand Jamboree **高中部**、**大學部**與**研究生部**前十名共三十支隊伍「實際上
怎麼做事」的研究：用了哪條組裝路線、什麼才算一個結果、哪個模型是擬合出來的、哪個只是暫定值，以及
濕式與乾式實驗在什麼地方把東西交到對方手上。三個分組、各有總覽與橫向比較、每隊一頁，另有一區把三十
個 wiki 互相對讀，並整理成一份 2027 年的選題指南。全部讀自各隊 2026 年公開 wiki。中英雙語、無建置
流程、無相依套件。

## What this is, and what it is not

This repository contains **secondary analysis of thirty public wikis**. We measured nothing. Every
number is the team's own, quoted as they state it, and every team page links to the wiki it was
read from.

It is **not a ranking**. The official result is the official result. iGEM publishes the Grand Prize
and the runners-up in order and lists the rest of each Top 10 alphabetically; where no order was
published, we do not infer one. Where this site makes a *judgement* rather than reporting what a
wiki says — the measurement ladder, the failure taxonomy, the four archetypes, the three
currencies of proof, the 2027 selection gates, the "what we would copy" lists — it is labelled as ours on the page where it appears, so it can be disagreed with
precisely.

It is also **not an audit**. A wiki is a claim about what happened in a lab, written by the people
who were there and who wanted to win. We read the claims carefully; we did not reproduce them.

本專案收錄的是**對三十個公開 wiki 的二次分析**。我們沒有量測任何東西，每個數字都屬於產出它的隊伍。
本站**不做排名**；凡屬*判斷*而非轉述的部分，都在頁面上標示為我們的看法，以便讀者精確地提出不同意見。

## Pages

| Page | What is in it |
|---|---|
| Overview (per section) | The findings across that section's ten, the measurement ladder, failure as method, the workflow archetypes, and what we would take from it |
| Side by side (per section) | Chassis and assembly route, what each team decided a result was, the dry-lab stacks, a documented-practice matrix, and what each team shipped that another team can use |
| One page per team | The design decision · a wet/dry swimlane diagram · the wet-lab pipeline step by step · the measurement discipline · the model and software stack · what failed · what we would copy |
| Three tiers | All thirty wikis read against each other: field sizes and odds by section, country and village conversion, three currencies of proof, the one practice shared by all thirty, and what each tier should steal from the others |
| 2027 roadmap | A project-selection guide built from the thirty: five gates a candidate topic must pass, six project shapes with their evidence cost, a scoring sheet, a season calendar read backwards from the wiki freeze, and six anti-patterns |
| Five ideas | Five candidate 2027 topics of our own, each derived from an empty cell in one of the tables above, each put through the five gates, with the five we rejected and the gate that killed each one. Nothing on these pages has been run |
| One page per idea | The argument · the prior art and how far it got · the key data with every figure cited · the five gates answered · a wet/dry swimlane · tools and a bill of materials · a month-by-month schedule · what kills it · what it ships and which awards it maps onto |

Ideas 4 and 5 are built on the mechanisms Stanford-University and Aarhus-University used in 2026 —
ADAR-read RNA sensing and microRNA-gated activation — recast so that a High School team confined to
the iGEM White List can produce evidence for them. Sources for every official figure on those pages
are in [`notes/2027-idea-sources.md`](notes/2027-idea-sources.md).

Switch sections with the **Undergrad / Overgrad / High School / Across tiers** control at the left
of the tab bar. The section is carried in the page id (`#og-…` Overgraduate, `#hs-…` High School,
`#x-…` cross-tier), so every deep link keeps working.

## Structure

```
index.html          design system + shell (the only HTML file)
app.js              hash router, division switch, EN/中文 layer, content helpers
fig.js              figure engine: bars, columns, lines, matrix, funnel, flow, gantt, scatter, custom
wf.js               workflow diagrams: the wet/dry swimlane, the DBTL attachment ring,
                    the measurement ladder, the failure taxonomy
data/meta.js        site metadata, all three team rosters, the four sections and their tab orders
data/overview.js    the comparative analysis, Undergraduate
data/compare.js     the side-by-side tables, Undergraduate
data/t-<team>.js    one page module per Undergraduate team
data/og-overview.js the comparative analysis, Overgraduate
data/og-compare.js  the side-by-side tables, Overgraduate
data/og-t-<team>.js one page module per Overgraduate team
data/hs-overview.js the comparative analysis, High School
data/hs-compare.js  the side-by-side tables, High School
data/hs-t-<team>.js one page module per High School team
data/x-tiers.js     the cross-tier analysis across all thirty
data/x-roadmap.js   the 2027 project-selection guide
notes/              the raw working notes each page was written from, one file per team
tools/qa.py         checks every data file: no arrow functions, no Simplified characters,
                    matched en/zh key counts, every table-of-contents id has a section
```

Every page is a plain object with a `render(h)` function, and every string is a
`{ en: '…', zh: '…' }` pair. There is no framework, no bundler and no network call at runtime: open
`index.html` and it works.
