# Stanford University — Kele Patch (Therapeutics, USA) — **GRAND PRIZE, Undergrad 2026**
Title: Kele Patch: A mRNA logic-gated RADAR sensor for cell-state specific reprogramming in keloid scars
Wiki: https://2026.igem.wiki/stanford-university  ·  Software: RADAR-Scout, https://radar-scout.vercel.app/

## Core idea
Keloid scars grow past the wound and never regress; recurrence after excision is 45–100 %.
Therapy = **RADAR** RNA sensors (Kaseniit et al. 2023, Gao lab): a stop codon in the sensor
transcript is edited away by endogenous **ADAR** only when the target transcript hybridises, so
payload is made *only in cells with the keloid transcriptional state* — not in a tissue average.
Delivered as IVT mRNA from a **microneedle array patch**. Two triggers in tandem = an AND gate.

## WET LAB workflow
1. **Targets come from the dry lab**, not from literature browsing: RADAR-Scout picks
   POSTN, ASPN, ADAM12, COL11A1, SFRP4 → 5 sensors per gene = a **5×5 panel of 25**
   (later a 26-sensor panel).
2. Build: sensor inserts into the single-input RADAR backbone **EK438** (SFFV promoter,
   Gao-lab gift; EK574 = ADAR1 p150; triggers in backbone I043). Golden-Gate/Gibson style:
   KAPA HiFi PCR with 20 bp overhangs → DpnI digest → gel purify → **NEB HiFi assembly at 50 °C**
   → transform → sequence. One primer pair per fragment made three constructs because the
   backbones share flanks.
3. Test in HEK293: 24-well plates, **100,000 cells/well**, next-day transfection with
   **Lipofectamine 3000 + P3000, 500 ng total DNA per well**; each sensor run in **matched** and
   **mismatched** trigger conditions in the same transfection.
4. Readout = three-colour flow cytometry: **mCherry** constitutive from the sensor transcript
   (= sensor transfection), **mTagBFP** constitutive from the trigger, **EGFP** behind the edited
   stop codon (= activation). Also imaged on an EVOS-FL at ~6/12/24 h.
5. **Move off plasmid to mRNA**: templates generated by PCR rather than ordering T7 constructs —
   forward primer carries GC spacer + T7 promoter + ACAG spacer + Kozak + ATG + 17–22 nt of gene;
   reverse primer adds the poly(A). HiScribe T7 → DNase I → precipitate → **Vaccinia capping** →
   precipitate → poly(A) tailing → purify. Delivered with **Lipofectamine MessengerMAX, 500 ng/well,
   no P3000** (P3000 is only needed for nuclear delivery of DNA).
6. AND gate: pCC869 = POSTN C0 + ASPN C7 in tandem on the EK438 backbone.
7. Payload swap: **SuCas12a2** (from Addgene #257717, NLS-Tag-P2A-TagBFP) tested in a 50:50
   co-culture of HEK293T-eGFP and HEK293-mScarlet, 25,000 + 25,000 cells per well, with a
   targeting vs non-targeting guide — a collateral-cleavage payload instead of a protein.
8. Self-amplifying RNA: three architectures coupling the **VEEV** nsp1-4 replicase to the sensor
   (split two-strand vs single-strand placements) — designed and built, not yet working.
9. Primary cells: healthy dermal and keloid fibroblasts; transfection there was the hard part.
   Future step = **GenoSkin** human skin models.

## How they define a result (the most disciplined measurement section in the Top 10)
Eight metrics defined once, then used without re-explanation, split into **activation** (how many
cells turn on) and **output** (how much payload those cells make):
sensor/trigger expression level; sensor activation efficiency (% of mCherry+ that are EGFP+);
activation fold change (match ÷ mismatch); **output per sensor** = geometric-mean EGFP ÷
geometric-mean mCherry inside the **top-5 % mCherry gate**; output fold change; **leakiness** =
output in the mismatch condition.
Two explicit bars for calling a sensor functional, and the reason: *flow runs on different days are
not comparable* (instrument drift, passage number, transfection efficiency), so **every
match-vs-mismatch comparison is made within one transfection**, and "statistically significant"
requires three successful biological replicates with p < 0.05.
Replicate agreement on the panel: **r = 0.94**.

## DRY LAB workflow — RADAR-Scout, six modules
**1 Target selection that fits the technology.** Differential expression is rejected on principle:
a RADAR sensor reads single cells, so they score three populations — **P** (pathological cell type),
**H** (same cell type, healthy donor), **B** (bystander cell types in diseased tissue)*.*
Pipeline over **26 single-cell RNA-seq cohorts** (cardiac, cutaneous, renal, hepatic, pulmonary
fibrosis), **960,518 cells**, 1,728 donor-population observations, from CELLxGENE + GEO
(GSE163973, GSE181316) + the Human Dermal Fibroblast Atlas + their own RNA-seq.
Cross-organ check: top 300 genes per organ, 1,500 rank slots → **1,136 distinct genes, 857 in only
one organ** → fibrosis signatures are largely organ-specific.
Isoform check with **GTEx long-read data**: does the isoform carrying the trigger actually exist in
fibroblasts? Out-of-core filtering so the Gao lab's **~7 million candidate rows** never load into
memory (698 designs for ADAM12, 1,303 for COL11A1 is otherwise unmanageable).
**2 Nineteen predictors, ViennaRNA 2.7.2** (RNAfold / RNAplfold / RNAcofold) in six families:
sequence composition, sensor secondary structure, sensor accessibility, trigger secondary
structure, trigger accessibility, sensor–trigger hybridisation. Correlated against flow output.
**Finding that changed the field's own heuristic: folding energy, not GC content.** Sensor
partition-function ΔG reaches **Spearman ρ = 0.62–0.63**, while GC content sits near −0.2.
Folding free energy was then added to RADAR-Scout as the ranking filter.
**3 Mechanistic ODE/stochastic framework**, built because linear regression on the best predictor
gave only **Q² = 0.20**, and because reporter expression lagged transfection badly in primary
fibroblasts. Five linked models: (1) plasmid nuclear entry, (2) single trigger + plasmid,
(3) single trigger + RNA, (4) AND gate + plasmid, (5) AND gate + RNA. Shared lipoplex delivery
cascade from Ligon, Leonhardt & Rädler (2014): attachment k_A, wash-off k_W, endocytosis k_E,
endosomal escape k_L, endosome degradation d_E, unpacking k_U — with a **stochastic
multi-lipoplex occupancy** variant (pits/endosomes tracked by occupancy number, cap of 10
inherited from the mRNA model and flagged as an uncalibrated assumption).
Model 1 is **fitted, not assumed**: three free parameters (p_entry, k_tx, k_sat) optimised by
**simulated annealing in Python** against Cohen et al.'s qPCR nuclear-copy-number dose-response in
B16F10 → **p_entry = 10.7 % per division, k_sat = 232 plasmids per nucleus, k_tx = 0.273 h⁻¹**.
Nuclear entry is mitosis-gated with k_div = ln2 / T_doubling, and they state that k_div is not the
entry rate. **82 input parameters are tabulated with value, unit, source and a note on which ones
still need measuring** — including competitive binding for the shared ADAR pool in the AND gate.
**4 Fluorescence as a three-channel readout** modelled explicitly (immature/mature reporter steps),
so the AND gate is legible in the same channels the lab actually measures.
**5 Microneedle array patch**, starting from a DeSimone-lab saRNA-LNP design (8 × 5 mm, 16 needles,
0.5 mm base, 0.6 mm long) and lengthening the needles to **0.9 mm**, justified from both sides:
keloids are thicker and the dermal–epidermal junction must be crossed, but >1000 µm hits nerve
endings. Nociceptor model: a 3.0 mm needle crosses **30.75× more nociceptors** and gives
**4.46× (≈4.5×) more reported pain**. Second justification is mechanosensing — YAP/TAZ in
reticular-dermis fibroblasts are the stiffness-sensitive ones (Type I collagen, 100–20,000 Pa)
versus papillary (Type III, as low as <1 Pa).
**6 Diffusion FEA**: Fick's second law + Einstein–Stokes + Darcy (needle pressure), 1-D,
D_eff = 1.57 × 10⁻¹⁴ m²/s over 2.5 mm of dermis, with a hard deadline set by the drug itself —
the LNP PEG layer is gone in ≤6 h and bare mRNA has a ~5 min half-life, so **the drug must arrive
within 6 hours**. Modifications tested in the model: adding collagenase, and applying pressure.
**Plus** a **U-Net** computer-vision pipeline: photo of a scar → segmentation → 3D-printable STL →
a patch cut to that patient's scar geometry.

## Workflow signature
The tightest dry-lab→wet-lab→dry-lab loop in the Top 10. The model chooses the targets, the lab
builds exactly those, the flow data re-fits the model, and a published design heuristic (GC
content) is replaced by a better one (folding energy) with the correlation to prove it. Every
limitation is stated: no 3-D RNA, reporters not payloads, parameters that still need measuring.
